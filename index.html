01 / Behind the hello

A little
about me.

I'm Yi, currently based in Lyon, France, and pursuing a PhD at the CREATIS Laboratory, University of Lyon.

My studies have taken me from France to Korea and Japan. This page brings together a few stops along the way, some of my work, and a way to say hello.

On the work side, I study medical imaging and 3D deep learning as part of my PhD in Signal and Image Processing.
