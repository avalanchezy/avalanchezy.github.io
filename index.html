01 / About me

A little
about me.

I'm Yi Zhu, currently based in Lyon, France, and pursuing a PhD at the CREATIS Laboratory, University of Lyon.

I’m from Changzhou in Jiangsu, China. My studies have taken me to France, Korea and Japan. This page brings together a few stops along the way and some of my work.

On the work side, I study medical imaging and 3D deep learning as part of my PhD in Signal and Image Processing.
