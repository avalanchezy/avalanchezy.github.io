From city streets to dental scans and brain MRI, I study how to reconstruct, align and understand 3D data.

4 papers

01
ACCV 20263D reconstruction

MapPano3D: Map-Guided Large-Scale Urban Reconstruction from Panoramic Videos

Y Zhu, K Aizawa, S Valette, S Ikehata

MapPano3D uses a 2D road map to bring local reconstructions from panoramic street videos into a coherent urban scene. Road topology and surface cues guide alignment, then the same transforms place buildings, trees and sidewalks—using pretrained models without additional training.

MICCAI 2026 · ODIN ChallengeOralRegistration

Registration Teaches Registration: Transform-Derived Crown Guidance for Semi-Supervised CBCT--IOS Alignment

Y Zhu, R Kechichian, R Richert, S Valette

RTR matches dental CT volumes to detailed intraoral surface scans by turning a small set of known alignments into weak crown annotations and expanding training with unlabeled cases. Predicted crown regions guide geometric alignment, while learned ranking and consistency between the two jaws select the final transforms.

Read paper↗
03
ISBI 2026Poster3D reconstruction

High-Fidelity 3D Tooth Reconstruction by Fusing Intraoral Scans and CBCT Data via a Deep Implicit Representation

Y Zhu, R Kechichian, R Richert, S Ikehata, S Valette

We align and combine the detailed crown from an intraoral scan with the root from CBCT to form a rough hybrid model. For incisors, this model guides latent-code optimization in a pretrained DeepSDF shape model, replacing the rough join with a continuous, complete 3D tooth surface.

Read paper↗
04
ICSP 2024OralSegmentation

Comparative Analysis of Three Advanced Deep Learning Algorithms for Multiple Sclerosis Lesion Segmentation in FLAIR MRI

Y Zhu, T Grenier, C Revol-Muller

We compare nnU-Net, UNeXt and YOLOv9 for outlining multiple sclerosis lesions in FLAIR brain MRI. The study examines how volumetric segmentation, a convolution–MLP model and detection-based segmentation handle the same task, using expert lesion masks as a reference for evaluation.
